The owner asked the assistant to review the other sites in the Vercel account, share an assessment,
then choose and build a useful idea for review the next day. The requested homepage timer animation
was published first. The new product was deliberately kept separate from all existing sites.

The review covered nine other projects. Read-only inspection found:

- Landed presents the clearest focused utility; KiAnben provides a trade-service context. This is
  a product judgment, not customer-demand evidence. Tariff accuracy, licensing claims, pool activity,
  commercial metrics and live forms were not independently audited.
- Mindmap News works at its Vercel alias and opens a source/uncertainty-backed branch. Its custom
  news subdomain failed DNS resolution in both Chrome and a local DNS query. No DNS change made.
- GarmentMind's supplied demo signs in and processes its built-in matching example. Its promises
  of catching every mismatch and a result described as safe to present need qualification. Real
  extraction, bank acceptance and cross-account behavior were not tested.
- QuickBill renders an invoice editor with BDT available, though INR is the initial currency.
  FlowDeck persisted a sample task and note after its save delay. These were browser-local examples.
- The Social Agent public entry gate was inspected; private operations were not accessed.
- The old portfolio alias remains public with credentials and testimonials inconsistent with the
  confirmed profile. Proposed redirect/copy cleanup was not deployed. An older test project's alias
  returned deployment-not-found; it was not deleted.

## Prototype

Source: `experiments/shipment-desk/` in the active Personal Website workspace. Plain buildless
HTML/CSS/JS, with a separate owner-private ChatGPT Sites publication. Current published source
commit: `f032c018f5e399113844f8abc213159027d51517`. All existing Vercel sites and their domains were
left unchanged apart from the authorized timer release.

Shipment Desk holds a next action with owner/date, a manual stage, expected arrival, document
checklist, manually recorded payment deadlines in Tk, notes and a printable brief. It offers JSON
backup/import; duplicate imports retain existing records. Explicitly labelled sample shipments
are opt-in. Amounts use integer poisha. Source text is escaped; backup schemas/dates/limits are
validated. No contacts, account credentials or existing customer data were imported.

Records live in the browser, not a shared team database. No automated reminders, payments,
shipping feeds, document validation or communication integration. A checked item records the
operator's own action, not an independently verified bank/customs outcome.

30 model assertions and 22 browser workflow assertions passed; desktop/tablet/390px/320px layouts
have no measured horizontal overflow. A real JSON download was read back with three labelled sample
records; a PDF brief was produced. The native automation download helper falsely reported canceled
even for a control link, but ordinary browser clicking wrote the actual file. Private publication
succeeded and access inspection confirmed owner role, a single allowed user and zero external
visitors. Authenticated end-to-end use of the hosted copy on a physical phone was not verified.

## Product hypothesis, not a validated business

The proposed gap is the operational handoff between a quote/cost estimate and delivery/invoicing:
who needs to do what next? It may be useful as a companion to trade services. It has not demonstrated
willingness to pay or retention; WhatsApp and spreadsheets are plausible adequate substitutes.
The assistant's provisional standalone-business score was 4.9/10, not market evidence. Proposed
next step: three operators try real shipments for a week and report missed follow-ups and repeat
use before adding integrations or pricing. No experiment participants were contacted.

The private prototype includes `review.html`, the full dated nine-site assessment. Local evidence:
`verification/vercel-review-2026-11-06/`. The owner has not yet accepted the prototype.
